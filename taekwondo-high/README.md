## 📖 Overview & Narrative

**Taekwondo High: Belt of Fury** combines the lightning-fast pacing of classic arcade beat 'em ups (*Streets of Rage*, *River City Ransom*) with modern roguelike skill drafting, realistic 3D skeletal motion kinematics, and quantitative reasoning exam trials.

Step into the shoes of a determined freshman transfer student entering a notorious high school ruled by martial arts syndicates and tyrannical club leaders. To bring justice and balance to the academy, you must fight your way up through 10 distinct, messy, vandalized floors—from the graffiti-covered entrance lockers and chaotic cafeteria, through the locked library sanctuary and dangerous chemical labs, straight to the midnight storm of the rooftop for a showdown against **Head Shin** (colloquially called **"Beast Baldy"** by students).

### 🥋 Authentic 3D-Kinematic Motion Modeling
- **True WTF/ITF Taekwondo Kinematics**: Every strike is procedurally calculated with full anatomical fidelity: knee chambering $\rightarrow$ hip rotation $\rightarrow$ foot extension with the ball, heel, or instep $\rightarrow$ snap recovery.
- **High Mobility Jump System (`Space` / `Tab`)**: Experience fluid aerial freedom. Vault over sweeps, maneuver in 2.5D space, and rain down devastating airborne strikes (*Flying Side Kick, Axe Drop, 540° Tornado Kick*).
- **Grommet Parry & iFrame Slide Dash**: Neutralize enemy rushes with tight frame parries (`Q`) or slide-step through projectile barrages (`Shift`) with invulnerability frames.

### 🪑 Dynamic Interactive Environment
- **Kickable Heavy Study Desks**: Line up your front snap kick (`J`) behind heavy wooden reading desks to send them sliding across the floor, bowling through dense crowds of delinquents.
- **Haphazard Throwable Ammo System**: Scavenge cafeteria apples 🍎, prickly cactus pots 🌵, steel drafting rulers 📏, sharpened pencils ✏️, heavy textbooks 📖, and lethal shurikens ⭐ (`G`/`T`) to control the arena at range.

### 🧱 Bricky Lego Physics Defeat Effect
- When enemies and bosses succumb to your fury, they shatter into individual bouncy plastic toy bricks (heads, torso blocks, limbs, hands, shoe pieces, and 1x1 studs) simulated with realistic bouncing physics, gravity, rotational spin, and contact drop shadows.

### 💥 Unstoppable Signature Special Arts
- **Pa-dong Gwon (Hadouken Surge - `H` / `Y`)**: Unlocked at Red Belt. Channels 245 raw damage in an aerodynamic projectile composed of a blazing white core, radiant sapphire plasma rings, and vibrant coral aura trails, punctuated by a synthesized booming *"HADOUUUUKEN!"* battle cry.
- **Golden Dragon Overdrive (`I` / `F`)**: The ultimate Black Belt finisher. Summons a roaring Eastern Celestial Golden Dragon with razor-sharp saber fangs, stag antlers, glowing oral fire, and a whirlwind vortex of emerald leaves that obliterates the screen.

## 🥋 Belt Progression & Martial Arts Arsenal

Advance through 6 distinct belt ranks by harvesting XP from fallen foes. Each rank transforms your visual uniform and expands your offensive repertoire:

```
[ ⚪ White Belt ] ──► [ 🟡 Yellow Belt ] ──► [ 🟢 Green Belt ] ──► [ 🔵 Blue Belt ] ──► [ 🔴 Red Belt ] ──► [ ⚫ Black Belt ]
```

1. **⚪ White Belt (Novice Striker)**:
   - *Techniques*: Front Snap Kick (`J`), Back Heel Thrust (`K`), Basic Push Kick.
2. **🟡 Yellow Belt (Disciplined Student)**:
   - *Techniques*: Unlocks **Dollyo Chagi (Fast Roundhouse Kick)** on Special 1 (`L`).
3. **🟢 Green Belt (Gym Fighter)**:
   - *Techniques*: Unlocks **360° Hurricane Twister Kick (Twimyo Mondollyo 360)** (`U`) with vacuum AoE, and **Yeop Chagi (Side Thrust Kick)** (`L`).
4. **🔵 Blue Belt (Elite Prospect)**:
   - *Techniques*: Unlocks **Naeryeo Chagi (Crushing Axe Kick)** (`L`) and **Kihap Shockwave** (`U`).
5. **🔴 Red Belt (Grandmaster's Disciple)**:
   - *Techniques*: Unlocks **540° Tornado Spin Kick** (`L`) and **Pa-dong Gwon (Hadouken Surge)** (`H`).
6. **⚫ Black Belt (Supreme Grandmaster)**:
   - *Techniques*: Unlocks the celestial **Golden Dragon Overdrive (`I`)**, peak damage multipliers, and golden particle aura.

## 🧠 Mind & Discipline: 200-Question UTBK PK Master Database

True martial arts balance physical prowess with mental acuity. **Taekwondo High** embeds an authentic, officially formulated **200-Question 2026 UTBK Pengetahuan Kuantitatif (PK)** engine!

### 📊 Comprehensive Topic Breakdown
1. **Pola Barisan & Deret Bilangan**: Aritmetika, Geometri, Barisan Bertingkat, Deret Fibonacci, & Teleskopik.
2. **Aljabar Linier & Sistem Persamaan**: SPLDV, SPLTV, Faktorisasi Aljabar, & Nilai Simetris.
3. **Operator Khusus & Simbol Baru**: Evaluasi formula kustom ($\otimes, \#, @, \Delta, \star$).
4. **Persamaan & Pertidaksamaan**: Bentuk Kuadrat, Eksponensial, Bentuk Akar, & Logaritma.
5. **Statistika & Pengolahan Data**: Nilai Rata-rata Gabungan, Median, Modus, & Jangkauan Antarkuartil.
6. **Teori Bilangan & Keterbagian**: FPB/KPK, Teorema Sisa, Bilangan Prima, & Digit Satuan Siklis.
7. **Aritmetika Sosial & Kecepatan**: Laba/Rugi, Rasio Kerja Gabungan, & Jarak-Waktu-Kecepatan.
8. **Peluang & Kombinatorika**: Kaidah Pencacahan, Permutasi Unsur Sama/Siklis, & Kombinasi Acak.
9. **Matriks & Vektor**: Determinan $2 \times 2$, Invers Matriks, Sifat Transpose, & Perkalian Matriks.
10. **Fungsi Komposisi & Invers**: Evaluasi $(g \circ f)(x)$, Invers Rasional, & Daerah Asal (Domain).
11. **Geometri Bidang & Ruang**: Teorema Pythagoras, Sudut Berpelurus/Berseberangan, Luas Tembereng Lingkaran, & Volume Prisma.
12. **Analisis Kuantitas P dan Q**: Perbandingan matematis dua kuantitas berdasarkan kondisi yang diberikan.
13. **Kecukupan Data Pernyataan (1) & (2)**: Logika kecukupan data untuk menjawab pertanyaan tunggal.
14. **Pertidaksamaan Nilai Mutlak & Pecahan**: Penentuan himpunan penyelesaian garis bilangan.
15. **Kalkulus Dasar & Optimasi**: Turunan pertama fungsi, titik stasioner, serta optimasi biaya/keuntungan.

*Every question is paired with a comprehensive step-by-step Indonesian official explanation (**Pembahasan Resmi**) for seamless study and self-evaluation.*

## 🚀 Quick Start & How to Play

### 🌐 Play Directly in Browser
No downloads or complex installations required! Simply open `index.html` in any modern web browser:
- **Google Chrome** (Recommended)
- **Mozilla Firefox**
- **Apple Safari**
- **Microsoft Edge**

### 💻 Local Development Server
To run the game locally using a lightweight HTTP server:

```bash
# Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/joserafaelsiregar/taekwondo-high.git

# Navigate into the project directory
cd taekwondo-high

# Launch with Python 3 built-in server
python -m http.server 8080

# Or launch with Node.js npx serve
npx serve .
```

Open your browser and navigate to `http://localhost:8080`.
